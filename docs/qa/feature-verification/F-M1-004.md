## AC verification (HORO-835)

- **No-match/malformed/ambiguous cases fail closed according to
  documented semantics.**
  `policy_replay.rs::malformed_condition_with_unknown_field_tag_fails_to_deserialize`,
  `::malformed_policy_document_missing_required_field_fails_to_deserialize`,
  `::malformed_effect_value_fails_to_deserialize_rather_than_defaulting_to_deny`,
  `::document_with_unsupported_schema_version_is_never_evaluable_even_with_valid_rules`
  — every malformed-input path is a hard deserialization/validation
  error, never a silent default. Ambiguous (conflicting rule) and
  no-match cases were already covered by HORO-834's own
  `policy_decision.rs` (deny-overrides, `NoMatchingRule`,
  `IndeterminateEvidence`).
- **Spoof-prone signals cannot independently grant ALLOW.**
  `policy_spoof_signals.rs` composes multiple simultaneously-spoofed
  fields (uid + executable path both `SelfAsserted`, or one trusted +
  one spoofed) against a realistic two-condition policy and confirms
  none produce `Allow` — every condition on a rule must independently
  clear its own trust floor; partial trust never averages out to
  sufficient trust. `both_signals_trusted_and_correct_grants_allow`
  proves the policy is satisfiable at all, so the deny results are
  actually because of spoofing, not an unconditionally-unsatisfiable
  fixture.
- **Repeated evaluation is deterministic.**
  Already covered by HORO-834's
  `repeated_evaluation_of_the_same_inputs_is_byte_identical` and
  `rule_authoring_order_does_not_change_the_decision`. This ticket adds
  the replay dimension:
  `policy_replay.rs::a_policy_document_serialized_then_deserialized_produces_the_same_decision`
  and `::replaying_the_same_document_against_a_different_context_still_denies_correctly`
  confirm a document taken off the wire produces the identical decision
  the original in-memory document would have.
- **User examples are validated against the same fixtures where
  practical.**
  `docs/product/POLICY_EXAMPLES.md` publishes a worked policy example
  verbatim identical to the committed
  `crates/eltanin-core/tests/fixtures/example_policy.json`.
  `policy_replay.rs::example_policy_from_the_docs_matches_the_committed_fixture`
  loads that exact fixture via `include_str!` and validates all three
  documented outcomes (trusted-user allow, root deny, stranger default
  deny) — if the doc and fixture ever drift, this test fails on CI.
- **F-M1-004 verification record references evidence/docs.** This
  document.

## Known limitations (carried forward, not silently closed)

- **Full replay is not content-addressed.** `PolicyProvenance` names a
  policy by `policy_id` + `revision`, not a content digest — an author
  could edit a policy without bumping `revision` and a later "replay"
  under the same provenance could silently diverge. Documented in
  `docs/architecture/domain-model.md` since HORO-834; closing it needs a
  hashing dependency, out of scope for F-M1-004.
- **Single-condition `Allow` rules are permitted**, including rules
  satisfiable by `BestEffort`-trust evidence alone (e.g. `executable_path`,
  which is TOCTOU-raceable per `identity.rs`'s own documentation). This
  was flagged during HORO-834's independent review as a legitimate
  reading dispute over HORO-787's "UID... alone cannot imply ALLOW"
  invariant (operator-authored explicit rule vs. system-inferred trust)
  and resolved in favor of keeping single-condition rules writable — see
  HORO-834's Jira comment for the full reasoning. Not re-litigated here.
- **No `Principal`/role/group abstraction exists.** "Principal matching"
  (HORO-834's scope line) maps onto `uid`/`gid`/`executable_path`/
  `executable_hash` conditions directly, consistent with MVP 1.0 being
  single-host/single-session per `SECURITY_MODEL.md`.
- **No allocation/utilization signal exists to spoof.** HORO-835's scope
  mentions "spoof-prone... allocation/utilization signals," but no such
  condition exists in `Condition` at all (by design — see
  `docs/architecture/domain-model.md`'s list of deliberately unmatchable
  fields) — there is nothing to regression-test here because the surface
  is structurally absent, not merely defended at runtime.
